# Contributing to NeuroVision AI

Thank you for contributing to NeuroVision AI. To keep the project organized and efficient, please follow the guidelines below.

## Repository Structure

* `docs/` – Documentation, meeting notes, methodology, and reports
* `data/` – Dataset information and preprocessing records
* `notebooks/` – Jupyter notebooks for EDA and experiments
* `src/` – Model development, training, and evaluation code
* `reports/` – Results, figures, and performance summaries

## Branch Naming Convention

Please create a separate branch for your work.

Examples:

* `feature/dataset-selection`
* `feature/eda-analysis`
* `feature/baseline-cnn`
* `feature/documentation-update`

Do not work directly on the `main` branch.

## Commit Message Format

Use clear and descriptive commit messages.

Examples:

* `Added BraTS dataset documentation`
* `Completed initial EDA analysis`
* `Implemented baseline CNN model`
* `Updated methodology section`

## Pull Requests

Before merging:

1. Ensure your work is complete and tested.
2. Update relevant documentation.
3. Create a Pull Request (PR).
4. Request review from your Track Leader or Team Lead.
5. Merge only after approval.

## Communication

* Keep your assigned GitHub Issues updated.
* Inform your Track Leader about progress and blockers.
* Attend weekly meetings whenever possible.
* Ask questions early if you face difficulties.

## Track Responsibilities

### Track 1 – Data Engineering & Management

* Dataset collection and validation
* Data preprocessing and augmentation
* Dataset documentation

### Track 2 – Data Analytics & Validation

* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Data quality assessment
* Statistical analysis and visualizations

### Track 3 – AI Development & Research

* Model development
* Training and evaluation
* Performance optimization

### Track 4 – Research Documentation & Presentation

* Research writing
* Weekly reports
* Presentation and paper preparation

## Guiding Principle

Every contribution should help another track move forward. Collaboration, transparency, and consistent communication are essential to the success of NeuroVision AI.

Thank you for being part of the project.
